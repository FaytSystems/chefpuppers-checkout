Auto Shipping

Shipping adjusts by order size.

The Cloudflare checkout function counts your total bags and sends the matching shipping amount to Stripe Checkout.

  • 1–2 bags: $6.95 standard shipping.
  • 3–5 bags: $9.95 standard shipping.
  • 6–9 bags: $10.95 standard shipping.
  • 10+ bags: $19.95 standard shipping.
  • No free shipping threshold is applied.

Weekly Shipping

Shipments go out every Monday.

A set weekly shipping day helps keep inventory lean, bake fresher batches, and avoid rushing handmade orders out the door.

FAQ

Questions happy pup parents ask.

Are the treats made fresh?

Yes. ChefPuppers is built around fresh, homemade, made-to-order treat batches.

When do orders ship?

Orders ship every Monday as part of the weekly fresh bake-and-ship schedule.

What sizes are available?

Small dog, medium dog, and large dog treat options are available in 10, 20, and 50 treat bags.

How does the automatic bundle deal work?

Add four of the exact same bag to the cart. The Cloudflare checkout function automatically discounts one matching bag in Stripe Checkout.

Is checkout live?

Checkout is powered by Stripe-hosted Checkout through a Cloudflare Pages Function once your Stripe secret key is added in Cloudflare.
